# MD20 v0.1.0a3 — Windows installer (teaching preview)

Docking → system building → OpenMM molecular dynamics → SMD and umbrella sampling, on one computer.
This preview adds the new interface, the two teaching cases and the project-grade report.
The interface and the report are in English by default; Traditional Chinese is one click away (top right).

## Install (15–30 minutes, mostly downloading)
1. Download **`MD20-Windows-v0.1.0a3.zip`** below, right-click it and choose "Extract all".
2. In the extracted folder, **double-click `Install-MD20.cmd`**.
   - If SmartScreen appears, choose "More info" and then "Run anyway" (the script is not signed).
   - The program goes to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MD20` (about 6 GB; at least 8 GB free). An earlier version is not deleted; it is moved to `_to_delete`.
   - With an NVIDIA driver the CUDA platform is used, otherwise OpenCL.
3. Double-click **`MD20-GUI.cmd`** on the desktop. The interface opens at http://127.0.0.1:8020 (this computer only).

## What is new
- **Four modes on the home page**:
  - Teaching mode 1: several small molecules (NF-κB p65 paper).
  - Teaching mode 2: peptide (Keap1 paper).
  - Quick mode.
  - Professional mode.
- **Teaching pages**: the paper, background, learning objectives, the scientific checks the case demonstrates, what each step teaches, the parameters with reasons, and discussion questions. One click starts the case.
- **Case-specific parameter suggestions**, also in professional mode:
  - Every one of the 34 parameters starts from a value suggested for your case and goal, with the reason.
  - Every change is checked immediately (error, warning, note), with a one-click suggestion.
  - The run time is estimated from this computer's benchmark.
- **Screening**:
  - Example Excel table.
  - Clickable ranking chart.
  - Manual SDF upload when downloads are blocked, with strict file naming.
- **Confirmation before molecular dynamics** after the poses are chosen.
- **Peptide scientific checks**: cell penetration, and access to the binding site (size versus box before docking; burial and distance to the site after docking). These are notices and never stop the run.
- **Chain breaks** can be capped with neutral ACE and NME groups.
- **Ligands that coordinate a metal**: an opt-in distance-restraint fallback. That ligand gets no binding free energy, and the report says so.
- **Salt** type and concentration are explicit (sodium, potassium, lithium, rubidium or caesium with chloride, bromide, fluoride or iodide).
- **SMD window starts** come from relaxed-spring frames, with ringing diagnostics.
- **Report** (English or Chinese): every section explains what the step is, why it matters, the parameters used next to the recommendation, numbered figures and tables, and an interpretation written from the run's own numbers. It ends with a methods paragraph ready for a report, the limitations, a full parameter appendix and the decision log.

## Known limitations
- The interface is checked in CI by rendering every page in both languages; interactive use (clicks, uploads, a full run started from the interface) is confirmed on the laptop.
- Some older automatic explanations and the decision log are in Traditional Chinese; the English report shows them as recorded.
- The default pulling velocity (0.01 nm ps⁻¹) is under review after the laptop teaching run.
- Bonded zinc model: mean bond-length deviation close to the 0.3 Å tolerance; treat metal-site results as qualitative.
- The 1D centre-of-mass ΔG° is an approximation (labelled in the report).
